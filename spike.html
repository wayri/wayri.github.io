Supported analysis workflows

Supported workflows cover power and signal integrity, circuits, thermal studies, and selected EM/RF and mechanical analyses. Each workflow has its own model limits and external-engine requirements, listed in the solver status documentation.

SPIKE is an experimental preview. An available setup panel or imported model does not establish that a particular analysis is supported or validated.
