How it works, source & credits

Expand mode: components lift first, then the copper layers separate, the substrate fades and supported vias stretch. Selected nets shine through the board and follow the expanded geometry. Expand mode and net highlight guide.

This opt-in 0.8.0 website SDK uses an invisible shadow catcher, environment reflections and cached 1024-pixel shadows. The backdrop follows the site theme. Fast mode skips ambient occlusion, SMAA and supersampling, and caps the framebuffer at two million pixels (one million on constrained devices). Rendering pauses when idle or hidden. Frame rate depends on your device and source complexity.

await viewer.load('/designs/project.json', {view: '3d'});

No Node.js runtime, rendering server or downloaded HDR environment is needed. This is rasterized PBR presentation, not path tracing. Supplied models determine fidelity; missing models remain labelled stand-ins. Available in stable 0.8.0, with layer-colored full-net highlights and gold vias.
