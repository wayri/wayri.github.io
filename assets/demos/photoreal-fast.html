How it works, source & credits

This opt-in 0.8.0-preview.1 website SDK uses an invisible shadow catcher, environment reflections and cached 1024-pixel shadows. The backdrop follows the site theme. Fast mode skips ambient occlusion, SMAA and supersampling, and caps the framebuffer at two million pixels (one million on constrained devices). Rendering pauses when idle or hidden. Frame rate depends on your device and source complexity.

await viewer.load('/designs/project.json', {view: '3d'});
viewer.setRenderQuality('photoreal-fast');

No Node.js runtime, rendering server or downloaded HDR environment is needed. This is rasterized PBR presentation, not path tracing. Supplied models determine fidelity; missing models remain labelled stand-ins. The stable npm/CDN release remains 0.7.0.
