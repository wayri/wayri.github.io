Credits, evidence and display limits

The example is a public two-layer 30 × 15 mm board; SI uses the saved complete-channel derivative. Matching-PCB 2D and 3D overlays require the exact source PCB and a supported saved layer or elevation. Unknown values and unsupported placements are omitted. Grid preview cells are not clipped to copper or board cutouts. The independent 3D field view retains declared source coordinates and elevations and can be explored separately from the PCB.

Full result arrays remain in the numerical data browser and JSON download. Display selection and point limits remain visible. Local files stay in your browser; source paths inside results are never fetched. Review metadata before sharing.
