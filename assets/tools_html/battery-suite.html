Model equations, interpretation & limits

OCV is interpolated against accessible SOC. Pack resistance is Runit × series / parallel, and terminal voltage is Voc − I·R. Constant-power loads solve P/η = I(Voc − IR) on the low-current branch; an infeasible load ends service even if charge remains.

Reference charge is conserved across temperature changes. For a capacity factor f, the inaccessible lower charge window is Qref(1−f), and accessible SOC = (remaining reference charge − inaccessible window)/(Qref·f). Warming can make retained charge accessible; it does not create charge. This screening model needs a temperature curve appropriate to the actual cell, load and cutoff.

The model stops at the first cutoff, reserve, temperature-capacity, current or power constraint. Horizon/step-limited runs give a lower bound, not a predicted full lifetime. Very short pulses repeated over years may need a longer step budget or a separately justified averaged model.

Discharge only. No charge protocol, cycle-life prediction, Peukert/rate-capacity behavior, relaxation, hysteresis, transient polarization, self-heating, cell mismatch or protection dynamics. Fixed OCV/resistance and temperature factors do not establish accuracy across every current, chemistry or temperature. Primary cells must not be treated as rechargeable.
