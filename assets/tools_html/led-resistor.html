Math & Theory

Ohm's Law for LED:

$$ R = \frac{V_s - (n \times V_f)}{I_f} $$

Where $V_s$ is supply voltage, $V_f$ is forward voltage, $n$ is number of LEDs in series, and $I_f$ is desired current.

Power Dissipated by Resistor:

$$ P_R = I_f^2 \times R \quad \text{or} \quad P_R = (V_s - n \times V_f) \times I_f $$

Always choose a resistor power rating at least double the calculated dissipation for safety margins.
