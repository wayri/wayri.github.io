Math & Theory

Convection Coefficient (h): Estimated from airflow velocity $V$ using empirical correlations. $h \approx 10 + 5 \sqrt{V}$ (where V is m/s). 1 LFM = 0.00508 m/s.

Fin Efficiency ($\eta_f$): $\eta_f = \frac{\tanh(m H_f)}{m H_f}$ where $m = \sqrt{\frac{2h}{k t_f}}$.

Thermal Resistance ($R_{th}$): $R_{th} = \frac{1}{h(A_{base} + \eta_f A_{fins})}$. The total temperature rise is $\Delta T = P \times R_{th}$.
