Math & Theory

Transmission Line Transformers (TLT):

  • Optimal Characteristic Impedance: For a standard 1:4, Z0 = sqrt(Zin × Zout). For 1:9 Guanella (3 lines), Z0 = Zin × Nlines.
  • Guanella (Current): Focuses on providing high common-mode choking impedance. Excellent bandwidth and balance.
  • Ruthroff (Voltage): Uses bootstrap voltage addition. Tends to have less high-frequency bandwidth than Guanella due to phase delay.
  • Current at port: I = sqrt(P / Z)  |  Voltage: V = sqrt(P × Z)
