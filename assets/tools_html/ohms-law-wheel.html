The 12 Core Ohm's Law & Power Formulas

1. Voltage ($V$ in Volts): $$V = I \cdot R = \frac{P}{I} = \sqrt{P \cdot R}$$

2. Current ($I$ in Amperes): $$I = \frac{V}{R} = \frac{P}{V} = \sqrt{\frac{P}{R}}$$

3. Resistance ($R$ in Ohms): $$R = \frac{V}{I} = \frac{V^2}{P} = \frac{P}{I^2}$$

4. Power ($P$ in Watts): $$P = V \cdot I = I^2 \cdot R = \frac{V^2}{R}$$
