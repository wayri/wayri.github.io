Math & Theory

Statics: A simply supported beam uses static equilibrium $\sum F_y = 0$ and $\sum M = 0$ to solve for reaction forces $R_A$ and $R_B$.

Shear & Moment: Shear force $V(x)$ is the integral of the load function. Bending moment $M(x)$ is the integral of the shear force function. $V(x) = \int q(x) dx$, $M(x) = \int V(x) dx$.

Deflection: Based on Euler-Bernoulli beam theory, $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = \frac{M(x)}{EI}$.
