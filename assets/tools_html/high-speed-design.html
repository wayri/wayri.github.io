High Speed Design Guidelines

Crosstalk & Spacing

To minimize crosstalk, maintain a spacing of at least 3W (where W is trace width) between adjacent parallel traces.

Minimum Spacing (3W) 15 mil
Ideal Spacing (5W) 25 mil

Length Matching & Skew

Calculate allowable length mismatch based on timing skew requirements and propagation delay.

Prop. Delay (t_pd) 143 ps/in
Max Length Mismatch 70 mil

Via Stub Resonance

Calculate the resonant frequency of an unused via stub (causes deep signal nulls).

Resonant Frequency (f_null) 35.0 GHz
If f_null is close to the signal's Nyquist frequency, backdrilling is required.

Math & Theory

Theory: High-speed signals require controlled impedance routing to minimize reflections. The critical length is defined by the signal rise time, typically \(L_{crit} = \frac{t_r}{2 \times t_{pd}}\).
