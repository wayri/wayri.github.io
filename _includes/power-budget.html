How this estimate works

Average load = Σ(on-state power × duty cycle). Battery draw = average load ÷ converter efficiency. Runtime = usable battery energy ÷ battery draw. Peak load ceiling assumes every load with a nonzero duty cycle can turn on together; it is measured at the load, before converter losses.

The values are an editable example, not measurements from a project. This simple model assumes constant conversion efficiency and zero off-state power. Enter usable energy after any battery derating; standby draw, temperature effects, startup surges, and changes in efficiency need a more detailed budget.
