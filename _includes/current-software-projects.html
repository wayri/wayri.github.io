WayriCAD (formerly KiWay)

KiCad plugins

Independently installable KiCad tools for PCB design and review. Quick PI checks DC voltage drop, current density and copper losses; Quick SI screens routed signals and return paths; QuickTherm studies declared dissipation and board temperatures. The suite also includes fanout, via stitching, BOMs, assembly variants and manufacturing checks.

SPIKE

Experimental preview

A local PCB and assembly simulation workbench bringing geometry, materials, study setup and results into one project. Supported workflows cover power and signal integrity, circuits, thermal studies and selected EM/RF and mechanical analyses, with 2D/3D inspection, reports and MCAD exchange. Model scope and external-engine requirements vary by workflow.

KiCad Online (formerly KiView / KiCad Visual Review)

VS Code / Browser

Review KiCad schematics and PCB layouts beside your code, with net and layer inspection, cross-probing, assembly data and visual revision comparisons. The Chromium companion brings supported review workflows to GitHub and GitLab pages and local projects.

Android companion
