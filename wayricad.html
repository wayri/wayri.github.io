Three questions, three tools

Follow the current, the signal, and the heat.

Quick PI

Inspect DC voltage drop, current density, and copper losses using saved copper geometry, source/load terminals, and explicit current assumptions.

Quick PI guide ↗

Quick SI

Screen a routed signal for delay, reflections, termination, and return-path information alongside its stackup and driver/load assumptions.

Quick SI guide ↗

QuickTherm

Explore declared dissipation and thermal paths, from lumped component estimates to supported steady-state and transient board models.

QuickTherm guide ↗

These are model-based engineering checks. Review materials, operating inputs, boundaries, omitted geometry, and convergence before relying on a result.

Take saved results into your website.

KiCad WebView can read retained Quick PI, Quick SI and QuickTherm JSON beside the matching saved PCB. Explore fields, waveforms, saved thermal frames and point histories; cross-highlight supported exported net/object identities. Exact source hashes gate 2D PCB registration. Independent 3D fields require declared elevation, and full numerical arrays remain available.

Export the portable companion with the original solve-time PCB fingerprint. The browser displays supplied results; it does not rerun or qualify a solver. Missing values and unsupported geometry remain explicit.
