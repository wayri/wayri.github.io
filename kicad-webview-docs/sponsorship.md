KiCad WebView is **free to use, including commercially, under MPL-2.0**. Voluntary sponsorship helps sustain the shared library; no payment or individual commercial-use approval is required.

## Help keep the viewer useful

Support goes toward browser compatibility, mobile performance, responsive and accessible controls, schematic/PCB/3D fidelity, documentation and regression checks. Public issues and release notes show the project's work and shipped improvements.

Individuals and teams are welcome to discuss supporting ongoing maintenance. Sponsorship does not purchase a particular feature, response time, delivery date or engineering certification.

## Sponsorship and paid help

Sponsorship supports shared development. Paid integration, customization or support with agreed deliverables is a separate service. You can use the library and report bugs without sponsoring or purchasing a service.

The MPL-2.0 license and separate hardware/example licenses remain unchanged. Sponsors do not receive control over project decisions or preferential security handling. Names and logos appear in project acknowledgements only with explicit permission; funding-provider visibility settings are separate.

Use the provider's secure forms for financial verification. Please do not post payment, banking, tax or identity information in GitHub issues.
