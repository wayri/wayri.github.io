## Development assistance

AI tools assisted with implementation, debugging, tests and documentation for KiCad WebView. The maintainer remains responsible for the software’s releases, review and correctness. This assistance is not independent engineering validation, a hardware test or a qualification of the designs shown in the viewer.

This acknowledgement concerns the viewer software. It does not describe how any upstream hardware design was developed or imply endorsement by its authors.

## Upstream software and hardware

KiCad WebView builds on credited software including Three.js and fzstd, with provenance for the supplied owner-authored geometry subset. Their authors and applicable notices are recorded in the [release files](/kicad-webview/docs/license/#notices-supplied-with-this-release).

The [public examples](/kicad-webview/#public-examples) credit OLIMEX for ESP32-P4-DevKit Rev D1 and the Marble contributors for Marble. Their hardware files retain their separate CERN licenses and source READMEs. The viewer’s MPL-2.0 grant does not replace those terms or establish hardware performance.

- [OLIMEX’s pinned ESP32-P4-DevKit README](https://github.com/OLIMEX/ESP32-P4-DevKit/blob/26705d36407a07324348927dfd30fbf4ffc1d94c/README.md) identifies the design and separates its hardware, software and documentation terms.
- [Marble’s pinned README](https://github.com/BerkeleyLab/Marble/blob/a426777d92c0f22a546d4740b419a3937e0c1f90/README.md) credits the Regents of the University of California/LBNL, Michał Gąska/WUT, Larry Doolittle/LBNL, BACI/GARD and the WUT/Creotech contributors. Refer to that source for the complete notices and CERN-OHL-1.2 terms.
