About Yawar

Engineering the Physical Layer.

"The physical layer is unforgiving—it demands absolute truth."

Electrical engineering & embedded systems

Power Electronics & PLM

github.com/wayri

I’m an electrical engineer working on power electronics, embedded systems, and the software that supports them. I’ve worked in and led small R&D teams, taking hardware from early requirements and system architecture through design, testing, and commissioning.

My experience includes electrical power systems for LEO satellites at Pixxel, battery management for ground-based energy storage, and grid-tied solar PV inverters at 10 kW and above. My part in that work spans circuit and PCB design, firmware, prototype bring-up, and preparation for manufacture. It also involves DFM/DFA, vendor coordination, and reliability analysis through FMECA, FMEA, and worst-case circuit analysis.

I also build the equipment and software needed to understand and verify those systems: electrical ground support equipment, hardware-in-the-loop setups, power loggers, and protection instrumentation. My desktop and embedded software work covers data acquisition, load analysis, billing, and engineering calculations. I’ve also advised on spacecraft components and energy infrastructure, working with engineers, researchers, and vendors.

I’m interested in how the engineering decisions fit together across a product’s development. I enjoy working through trade-offs with a team, sharing what I’ve learned, and helping an idea become something useful. I’m also open to collaborations with engineers and researchers on hardware and tools that make day-to-day engineering work easier.

As part of a wider sourcing effort, contributed to work that reduced selected lead times from >6–7 months to 1–4 weeks. Contributed to FMECA, FDIR design, and firmware requirements, with work informed by MIL-STD-431G, ISRO PAX, IPC class 2/3, and ECSS standards.
