Power Electronics & PLM

github.com/wayri

I work on hardware and software across power electronics, embedded systems, test equipment, and space applications.

My work spans the product lifecycle: translating requirements into system architecture, designing circuits and PCBs, developing firmware, bringing up prototypes, and preparing hardware for manufacture and test. This includes DFM/DFA, vendor coordination, and reliability analysis through FMECA, FMEA, and worst-case circuit analysis.

Recent projects include electrical power system (EPS) architecture for LEO satellite constellations, battery management for ground-based energy storage, and grid-tied solar PV inverters at 10kW and above. I also develop electrical ground support equipment (EGSE), hardware-in-the-loop setups, power loggers, and protection instrumentation to help test and understand these systems.

Alongside hardware, I build desktop applications and embedded software for data acquisition, load analysis, billing, and engineering calculations. My experience also includes technical advisory work on spacecraft components and energy infrastructure, working with other engineers, researchers, and vendors from early design through testing and commissioning.
