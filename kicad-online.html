Android companion · Preview

KiCad review on a phone or tablet.

Formerly Pocket KiView, the Android companion includes review workflows for imported designs, documents, engineering calculators, BOM/iBOM data, saved simulation results, and Gerber analysis. Available tools depend on the host and imported data.
