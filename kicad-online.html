Follow the design, keep the context

Cross-probe the saved design

Move between schematic, layout and 3D. Follow nets across loaded sheets, inspect exact pins and pads, and open linked detail views with independent cameras.

Review where the finding belongs

Keep point, area, property and BoM notes tied to a drawing and revision. Browse supported Issues and Discussions, send replies explicitly and read provider updates back into the viewer.

Website-session operations use your existing repository access. Organization SSO and provider policy still apply; local notes do not post automatically.

Inspect assemblies and results

Review grouped parts and assembly state, fabrication layers and saved ERC/DRC reports. Fast DC drop accepts two contacts on the same saved net, voltage and load directly in Layout or 3D.

Fast DC drop is experimental analysis. Saved ERC/DRC and simulation reports are displayed; their native engines do not run in the viewer.

Review KiCad simulation workbooks

Run the circuit in KiCad, save the workbook, and export the traces you want to share. Keep the workbook and numerical exports in the same project.

  1. Open Simulation and select the workbook in Result file.
  2. On each workbook tab, choose the matching CSV, TSV, or ASCII RAW under Saved result.
  3. Choose signals, pan or zoom, and place Cursor A and Cursor B to compare saved samples.

To see a schematic’s built-in drawing sheet and title block, open the schematic and press Home.
